08 / CLARITY FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

Common questions regarding eligibility, registration on Unstop, and open greens logistics.

Anyone! Whether you are an undergraduate or postgraduate student (engineering, design, management), recent graduate (batches 2024–2026), working software developer, UI/UX designer, or self-taught creator residing in or affiliated with any district across Uttar Pradesh. Guest participants from outside UP are also welcome under the Guest Participant quota.

Registration is 100% free of charge and handled officially on Unstop. Thanks to Riwa Creatives and ecosystem sponsors, all shortlisted offline lawn finalists receive complimentary access to mentors, Wi-Fi, solar workstations, and meals.

At our flagship offline venues in Lucknow and Greater Noida, we have arranged open-lawn garden workstations equipped with high-speed mesh Wi-Fi, solar battery stations, and low-glare night lighting so you can code comfortably in nature under open skies.

You can participate solo or in squads of 1 to 4 members. If you are flying solo, join our official Discord and WhatsApp channels where dedicated matchmaking threads are actively pairing coders with designers.

Online registrations remain open through December 31, 2025. Preliminary reviews and virtual mentorship sprints run until January 8, 2026. The In-Person Grand Finale will be hosted on Sunday, January 11, 2026 across our open greens venues in Lucknow and Greater Noida.

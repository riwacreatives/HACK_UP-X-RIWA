* **Kicker:** `03 / TRACK ARENAS • विषय क्षेत्र`
* **Heading:** `Five Frontier Problem Spaces`
* **Subtext:** `Choose your domain. Build solutions addressing regional and national transformation.`

### Track 01: AI & GenAI for Bharat
* **Focus:** Vernacular Language AI, Multilingual LLMs, Indic Voice Assistants & GovTech
* **Description:** Harness the latest advances in Generative AI, multimodal vision models, and Indic linguistic datasets to empower Bharat. ### Track 02: Heritage, Craft & Tourism 2.0 (ODOP)
* **Focus:** One District One Product (ODOP), Artisan Digitization & Cultural Preservation
* **Description:** Uttar Pradesh is the cultural heartbeat of India—from Varanasi silk weaving and Bhadohi carpets to Kannauj perfumery and Moradabad brassware. Build next-gen digital provenance systems, direct artisan-to-global commerce platforms, AR/VR virtual temple and ghat experiences, and AI-powered authenticity verifiers.
* **Tag Pills:** `ODOP Artisans`, `Provenance`, `Spatial Heritage`, `Tourism 2.0`, `Craft Commerce`

### Track 03: Smart AgriTech & Rural Logistics
* **Focus:** Mandi Telemetry, Cold Chain Logistics, Kisan Telemetry & Drone Analytics
* **Description:** Power the agrarian spine of Uttar Pradesh. Build real-time APMC mandi price arbitrage dashboards, hyper-local microclimate weather prediction, farm IoT sensor networks, soil nutrient diagnostics via computer vision, and supply chain tracking that minimizes crop wastage from farm gate to urban markets.
* **Tag Pills:** `Mandi Telemetry`, `Farm IoT`, `Drone Analytics`, `Kisan AI`, `Supply Chain`

### Track 04: Creative Media & Spatial Web
* **Focus:** Interactive WebGL, Spatial Web, Cultural Storytelling & 3D Interactive Media
* **Description:** Combine high-octane engineering with artistic aesthetics. Craft blistering-fast client-side WebGL experiences, interactive 3D simulations, real-time creative canvases, and typography-rich cultural media that challenge standard web paradigms.
* **Tag Pills:** `WebGL / 3D`, `Creative Coding`, `Spatial UI`, `Generative Media`, `Interactive Canvas`

### Track 05: Open Innovation & Civic Tech
* **Focus:** Citizen Grievance Redressal, Smart Municipal Telemetry, Green Energy & Public Safety
* **Description:** Have a game-changing idea outside the defined tracks? This open arena invites bold, unconventional hacks—from civic reporting and potholes/road quality telemetry to emergency rapid response, clean Gomti waste tracking, and decentralized public goods infrastructure.

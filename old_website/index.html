08 / FAQs • अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about eligibility, logistics, and open greens participation.

Eligibility

Who can register for Hack Uttar Pradesh?

Anyone! Whether you are a college student, working developer, independent UI/UX designer, or self-taught creator residing in or affiliated with any district across Uttar Pradesh, you are welcome. Guest participants from outside UP are also invited.

Students Eligible Working Developers 75 Districts Open
Cost & Fees

Is there any registration cost or hidden fees?

Registration and participation are 100% free of charge. Thanks to Riwa Creatives and ecosystem sponsors, all participants receive free access to mentors, workshops, and meals for offline lawn finalists.

100% Free Entry Complimentary Food Cloud Allowances
Venue

What is the open greens coding experience?

At our flagship offline venues in Lucknow and Greater Noida, we have arranged open-lawn garden workstations equipped with high-speed mesh Wi-Fi, solar battery stations, and low-glare night lighting so you can code comfortably in nature under open skies.

Outdoor Lawn Workspaces Solar Battery Hubs Starlit Night Coding
Teams

Can I participate solo or do I need a squad?

You can participate solo or in squads of up to 4 members. If you are flying solo, join our official Discord server where dedicated matchmaking channels are actively pairing coders with designers.

Solo or Teams (1-4) Discord Matchmaking Inter-District Pairing
Schedule

What are the key dates and deadlines?

Online registrations remain open through December 31. Preliminary reviews, idea submissions, and virtual hack sprint rounds run until January 8. The Grand Offline Finale will be organized on January 11, 2026 across our flagship open greens venues in Lucknow and Greater Noida.

Registration till Dec 31 Online Rounds till Jan 08 Grand Finale (Jan 11, 2026)
